=== FINANCIAL RESULTS ===
Revenue: $1.57B (+40% YoY, vs $1.13B Q2 FY2025)
Adjusted EPS: $1.64 (+290% YoY)
Gross margin (non-GAAP): 44.9% (+3.9pp YoY)
Operating margin (non-GAAP): 19.5% (+11.3pp YoY)
EBITDA (non-GAAP): $341.8M (+192.9% YoY)

Revenue by segment:
- Optical Networking: $1,099.8M (70.0% of total, vs 68.7% prior year)
- Routing and Switching: $174.2M (11.1%)
- Platform Software: $93.9M (6.0%)
- Global Services: $179.4M (11.4%)

Customer concentration: Two customers >10% of revenue = 34.0% combined

FY2026 raised: $6.3B ±$100M (+32% YoY at midpoint); non-GAAP gross margin 44.5-45%; op margin ~19%

=== BACKLOG & SUPPLY-DEMAND ===
Backlog: $7.7B (record), QoQ +$600M from Q1 FY2026
"Management expects backlog to reach even higher levels by year-end"
"We continue to see an imbalance of supply not keeping pace with demand"
"As soon as we get them [components], they're going out the door"
Supply constraints: modem/CDM shortages, ongoing pump laser shortages
80%+ of hardware backlog converts to revenue within 12 months → visibility into 2027

=== HYPERSCALER / AI DEMAND ===
"The largest hyperscalers have increased their 2026 capital expenditures with indications of continued expansion into 2027 and beyond"
"An increasingly larger proportion of that spend will be directed towards network infrastructure" for AI workloads including "model training, data ingestion and inference"
DCI (data center interconnect) gaining momentum — customers connecting AI data centers to monetize investments

=== KEY TECHNOLOGY WIN ===
"Awarded the industry's first multi-rail opportunity using Ciena's RLS Hyper-Rail solution to connect training data centers over distance"
→ Hyperscaler multi-rail design win confirmed
