"1.6T to be beneficial to transceiver gross margin"
"EML and SiPho 1.6T transceivers offer similar gross margin profiles"

=== NVDA STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP ===
"NVIDIA's $2 billion equity investment in Coherent, Inc. and a multiyear supply agreement extending through the end of the decade"
Agreement covers "multiple CPO-related products including our high-power CW laser"
→ COHR becomes NVDA's primary CPO supplier

=== CPO TIMELINE ===
Orders reaching into CY2028, LTAs (long-term agreements) to end of decade
→ CPO timeline pulled forward from "2028-2030" to active ramp now

=== InP CAPACITY EXPANSION (pump laser relief) ===
"Double internal indium phosphide output capacity by end of calendar 2026"
"More than double again by end of calendar 2027"
→ 4x InP capacity over 2 years (2026-2027)
→ Pump laser shortage has a defined relief timeline

=== SUPPLY CHAIN ===
"Most of the components that go into our transceivers are internally sourced, which provides a buffer against external price increases"
→ Vertical integration shields COHR from upstream cost pressure
→ But this also means CIEN's pump laser shortage depends on COHR's own expansion pace
